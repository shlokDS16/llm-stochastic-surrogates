Generated by `analysis/pilot/side_by_side.py` from existing outputs only (no model run). Source facts: the earlier run directories listed in `config.json` (sha256). ## 1. Status of the two tables
- `table_a1.csv`, `paper/tables/pilot_a1.tex`: **preregistered (A1)** analysis. This is the primary analysis (A2.1 says A2 is reported next to it and is not called primary).
- `table_a2.csv`, `paper/tables/pilot_a2.tex`: **post-results (A2, not preregistered)**. Chosen after the first analysis was seen (A2 timeline). Never presented as primary.

## 2. Denominators
- 6 models x 7 paradigms = 42 model x paradigm grids; each A1/A2 grid is complete (no undefined cell). Cells per grid: C = 16 (P2a-d, P3, P4) or 32 (P1, with option order O); n = 50 sampled responses per condition per cell (design), fewer after invalid responses are dropped.
- Human side: 36 ML1 sites per paradigm (N: P1 6,271, P2a 5,362, P2b 5,284, P2c 5,627, P2d 5,609, P3 6,330, P4 5,942).
- Validity (column `validity`): share of sampled responses that parse AND finished (A1.10), over all sampled responses of the grid.

## 4. Preregistered interpretation rules (quoted) and how they were applied
- Stable: "sign stability >= 0.9 and the 95 percent bootstrap interval of Delta-bar - 2S (a conservative lower range of cell effects) stays on the canonical side of zero" (section 6; A1.4 replaces the bootstrap by Delta-bar_lo - 2 S_hi, "conservative, combined from separate bounds, not a joint interval"). Applied as sign stability >= 0.9 and `dbar_minus_2S_lower` > 0.
- Fragile: "sign stability < 0.6, or Delta-bar - 2S and Delta-bar + 2S lie on opposite sides of zero". Applied to the point estimates (literal reading).
- Neither label: the rules name no third class; rows that are neither stable nor fragile are labelled `neither`.
- Spread: "'Larger' or 'smaller' between LLM and human spreads is stated only when the intervals do not overlap, labelled as a conservative descriptive rule, not a significance test." Any such sentence must name the two sources (prompt perturbation versus site and sample differences) in the same sentence.
- "The human-tau comparison is described only as placing LLM prompt-spread on the scale of human between-site spread; no claim that one system is 'more variable' in a general sense."
- Factor shares: descriptive allocations, reported only when the lower bound of S^2 exceeds 0; not causal contributions (section 6, A1.5).
- A1.5: with n = 50 and 16 cells the pilot cannot distinguish S near 0.1 from 0.

## 8. Figure inputs (`fig_inputs/`)
- `fig11_forest.csv`: one row per model x paradigm x scale (A1_preregistered, A2_post_results; the `scale_status` column says which): LLM mu and S with intervals, human mu, tau (A1 only), S^H with intervals, rule outcome, flags. Draw A1 as the main panel; A2 as a separate, labelled panel.
- `fig12_variance_terms.csv`: **available.** The analysis jsons store, for every grid and both scales, the bias-corrected sum of squares SS_t of every term (W, F, T, [O], and all interactions) with its 95 percent interval, df, the total (= C x S^2 untruncated; checked), and the share where reportable (A1.5: only if the S^2 lower bound is above 0; NA with reason otherwise). These are corrected sums of squares of an enumerated set of cells, not estimated variance components (section 5: "no variance components are estimated"); shares are descriptive allocations; a corrected SS (and so a share) can be slightly negative because the sampling-variance correction is subtracted (kept as computed, not truncated). Shares are reportable in 29 of 42 A1 grids.
- `fig13_sign_cells.csv` (one row per perturbation cell: factor levels, d, v, 95% Wald limits, sign, canonical-sign-and-CI-excludes-0 indicator; their mean reproduces the stored sign stability, checked) and `fig13_sign_factor_levels.csv` (per factor level: share of cells with positive sign, share with canonical sign and CI excluding 0, share with the opposite sign and CI excluding 0). **Available** for A1 and A2.
- Nothing needed for figures 12-13 is missing. Caveats: for ND-flagged grids the A1 cell effects are g on a floored SD (A2.2); the 'W' levels W0-W3 are the frozen wordings, 'T' = 0.7 and 1.0, 'F' = F0/F1, 'O' = option order for P1 only.

## 9. Missing (NA) and why
- Human tau (REML) on the A2 raw scale: NA. metafor was run only on the A1 scales (`metafor_results.json`); only S^H exists on the raw scale. A new metafor run would be needed.
- Human mu and tau for A1 P2 use the `sens_log` rows (g on log answer), because the benchmark's `primary` P2 row is raw g, which the A1.2 LLM metric does not use.
- Human P1 raw-scale (risk difference) interval for the pooled effect: not in any earlier output; recomputed here with `estimators.estimate2` ('diff') on `data/processed/ml1/P1.csv`, seed 20261016; the point value reproduces `rawscale.csv` human_raw_dbar. Only this interval is new; all other human numbers are read.
